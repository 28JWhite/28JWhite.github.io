About PokeMod

PokeMod is a work in progress idea of allowing people to upload custom Pokemon, which people can download and add to their own games. In the future I also want to add the ability to add 3D Pokemon but currently I feel that it is too far out of reach and that there is not enough demand for it. Development began April 28th, 2026.
